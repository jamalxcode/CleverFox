About this app
arrow_forward
Getting better grades just got easier.
uLesson is a free educational learning app for primary, secondary school learners and candidates preparing for WAEC , GCSE , A levels , BECE , GCE , NECO , JAMB and other National exams .
With a perfect blend of highly engaging videos and personalised learning, uLesson leverages best in-class teachers, media, and technology to help students learn, understand and practice concepts in an easy, fun and engaging manner.
Download now and join thousands of learners who trust uLesson to learn and understand difficult subjects easily, helping them improve their school grades.
Key Features
A vast library of curriculum-relevant lessons for primary and secondary school learners.
Personalised learning experience so you can learn at your own pace. Watch lesson videos at your convenience, pause and rewind at will.
Get instant, personalised help with homework with the AI-powered homework help feature.
Challenge and defeat other learners around the world to top the global and local scoreboard with Multiplayer Quiz.
18,000+ interactive quizzes and tests with solutions to help students perfect their understanding.
Practice Exams with step by step solutions.
Periodic Mock Exams to help learners get confident ahead of real school exams.
Learning analysis dashboard to track progress and monitor performance.
Receive weekly report via SMS/WhatsApp to know how your child is progressing academically.
Please note that for an optimal experience on uLesson, it is recommended to use a device with a minimum of 2 GB RAM .
