Frequently Asked Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between Liner, Liner Scholar, and Liner Write? All three products are services built on precise AI search technology, but each has optimized features for different purposes.
Liner - AI search for quickly finding reliable information
Liner Scholar - Academic research AI for paper-based research
Liner Write - AI for writing content ready for professional use
Rather than spending time learning to master AI, we designed these tools so you can choose the right AI for your purpose and get results faster.
Do I need to subscribe to Liner, Liner Scholar, and Liner Write separately? No. You can use all of them with one subscription. When you subscribe to Liner Pro or Liner Max, you can use all three products together at no additional cost. Feel free to search, research, and write within your plan&#x27;s usage limits.
I&#x27;m planning on publishing blog posts every week. Which should I use, Liner or Liner Write? You can find information based on Liner’s search and verify the sources at the same time, so you can complete everything from research to writing in one go. If you want to focus directly on writing without having to search and organize information separately, Liner Write is the better choice.
Can I also write and search in Liner Scholar? Yes, Liner Scholar also supports both writing and searching. It&#x27;s particularly well-suited for research-focused tasks like academic paper-based writing or literature review. You can seamlessly progress from research to writing using the document editing features.
Can I use it on mobile? Currently, Liner Write and Liner Scholar are only supported on desktop environments. Because both products are optimized for tasks that require focus such as writing and research, they are most convenient to use on larger screens.
