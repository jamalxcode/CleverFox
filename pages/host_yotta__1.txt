Frequently Asked Questions
What are the advantages of Bare Metal over virtualized solutions?
Bare Metal servers deliver uncompromised performance by providing direct access to physical hardware with zero virtualization overhead. With complete hardware isolation, root-level control, and deterministic low-latency I/O, they are engineered for mission-critical workloads that demand raw computational power and security - such as AI/ML training, high-performance computing (HPC), large-scale data analytics, and latency-sensitive financial applications. Bare Metal infrastructure also ensures strict tenant separation, making it well-suited for compliance-driven environments requiring auditability, data sovereignty, and enhanced workload isolation.
Can I scale my Bare Metal server resources as my demand grows?
Yes, you can seamlessly scale your resources by adding additional nodes with GPUs as your workload requirements increase. If centralized storage is needed, you can integrate high-speed or object storage solutions based on the data type - whether it’s hot (frequently accessed) or cold (infrequently accessed) data.
Can I migrate my existing workloads to Shakti Cloud Bare Metal servers?
Yes, Shakti Cloud offers a streamlined platform to help you migrate your existing workloads to our Bare Metal infrastructure with minimal downtime. To begin, you can provision S3-compatible object storage and initiate your data transfer. Once the migration is complete, the data can be moved to High-Speed Storage (HSS), after which you can launch your Bare Metal instances with the volume attached. This approach supports efficient collaboration, especially if you’re working with multiple nodes that require shared data access. Object storage can also be leveraged for archiving or storing cold data over the long term
For whom is Bare Metal the ideal choice?
Enterprises training foundation models or LLMs | AI-first startups needing raw performance | Research & academia running large simulations | Government & defence requiring sovereignty
How is Bare Metal GPU different from standard cloud GPU instances?
Unlike standard cloud VMs that use virtualization, Bare Metal GPU nodes on Shakti Cloud provide direct access to physical NVIDIA GPUs (H100 SXM5 or L40S). This eliminates performance overhead, noisy neighbours, and throttling, ensuring maximum throughput and lowest latency for AI/ML workloads.
What are the ingress and egress charges associated with my BareMetal?
We offer unlimited free ingress and egress to support running your workloads at scale.
Can I add or remove Bare Metal nodes?
Yes. You can scale nodes up or down as required. For long-term contracts, scaling terms depend on capacity reservations, while short-term contracts allow flexible node scaling.
