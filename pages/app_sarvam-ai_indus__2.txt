Models
Every model is trained and evaluated on Indian languages, accents, and code-mixed
usage, not adapted from an English-first baseline.

codemix output modes.

and speaker.

November 2026 Knowledge Base Metadata Fields Define your own fields on a knowledge base, such as department, year or document type, and fill in
the values as you upload each file. Fields can be required, limited to allowed values, or filterable.
Read the changelog → October 2026 Larger Knowledge Base Files Add files up to 5 GB to a knowledge base. Files that are too large or the wrong type are now
flagged with a clear message instead of disappearing without explanation.
October 2026 Agent Session History Agent pages now have a Mine / Team toggle, so you can see your own recent sessions or those of
your teammates. Any session opens as a read-only transcript you can review after the run finishes.
October 2026 Bulbul V3 End of Life for Voice Agents Bulbul V3 reaches end of life on Voice Agents on November 10, 2026. Switch your agents to
Bulbul V4 before then.
