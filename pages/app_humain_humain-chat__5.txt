3. Full Interaction Logging and Human Review
Every Input and Output is recorded. Automated systems and authorized HUMAIN reviewers may analyze any interaction. Authorized reviewers working for HUMAIN's model technology provider may also receive and review interactions under applicable contractual, confidentiality, access-control and data-protection requirements.
Review may support safety, security, quality, Arabic and multilingual evaluation, regional or cultural alignment, abuse response, troubleshooting, support, benchmarking, training and further model development. Research Access interactions may receive heightened review because HUMAIN's filtering-layer safeguards may be reduced or removed.
Do not submit personal data or confidential, privileged, sensitive or regulated information. If you submit another person's data, you must have lawful authority and provide any required notice. HUMAIN may remove or restrict content that should not have been submitted.
4. Electronic Consent and Voluntary Research Opt-In
Contract acceptance, logging and review consent, training and development consent, and the Research Access opt-in or heightened-risk acknowledgement are presented as separate, unticked electronic controls. HUMAIN records the text or version, purpose, time, account and withdrawal status.
Participation in the Preview is voluntary. The disclosed recording, review and model-improvement activities are integral to the Preview, so HUMAIN will not enable new interactions unless required current consents are active. Marketing consent is separate.
Research Access is an additional voluntary option. Declining or leaving Research Access does not by itself end eligible Guarded Access. Any adult user may request Research Access and may be selected after HUMAIN verification; professional research credentials and a formal test plan are not required.

6. International Processing and Transfers
HUMAIN and its service providers may process personal data in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and other countries in which they operate or provide approved infrastructure or support. The specific processing and storage locations applicable to your access will be identified in the interface or a layered notice before collection begins.
Where personal data is transferred internationally, HUMAIN will use measures required by applicable law, which may include adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, transfer risk assessments, contractual safeguards, security controls and regulatory approvals. You may request information about applicable safeguards through the privacy channel.
7. Retention and De-identification
Data
Retention approach

Twelve (12) months from collection, then securely deleted or effectively de-identified unless longer retention is required by law or for a documented legal claim.
Selected effectively de-identified training data

Data already lawfully incorporated into trained model parameters may not be technically reversible or associated with an individual; HUMAIN will address requests according to applicable law and technical status.

9. Security and Incidents
HUMAIN uses risk-based technical, organizational and administrative safeguards, including access controls, reviewer authorization, logging, encryption where appropriate, environment separation and retention controls. No system is completely secure.
HUMAIN investigates and responds to personal-data incidents and provides notices to authorities or affected persons where applicable law requires. Promptly report suspected compromise through the privacy or security channel in the interface.
10. Cookies, Analytics and Communications
The Preview may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies for authentication, security, settings and session management and, where permitted or consented, analytics technologies to understand performance and use. Regional cookie controls will be presented where required.
HUMAIN may send service, security, access, consent, policy and activation notices through the interface, account, dashboard, email or another reasonable electronic channel. Marketing communications require any separate consent required by law and may be opted out of without ending service messages.
11. Children
The Preview is for adults aged eighteen (18) or older and is not directed to children. If HUMAIN learns that it collected personal data from a child through the Preview, it will take appropriate steps under applicable law.

HUMAIN may update this Notice to reflect changes in the Preview, processing or law. The updated Notice will identify its date. HUMAIN will provide additional notice or obtain fresh consent where applicable law requires.
