Unified, OEM-Agnostic AI Compute for Enterprise Innovation
World's first AI compute platform which unifies NVIDIA, AMD and INTEL GPUs using Red Hat OpenShift. We build enterprise grade, production ready, customized AI solutions, that makes AI work for our customers.
What is GPU-as-a-Service?
NxtGen offers unified GPU compute by abstracting NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel GPUs into one platform, while GPUaaS delivers on-demand, scalable access, eliminating hardware ownership, lowering costs, accelerating AI deployment, and maximizing resource efficiency.
Why Choose NxtGen for GPU Infrastructure
OEM-Agnostic GPU Platform
NxtGen supports NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel GPUs, ensuring compatibility across diverse AI workloads, from training and inference to analytics and visualization, while giving enterprises flexibility, performance optimization, and freedom to choose the right GPU for every use case.
Deployment for any AI Use Case
NxtGen enables customizable AI deployments for any use case through SAS-F (Patent Pending), allowing enterprises to train and run inference on their own data while flexibly tailoring models to domain-specific requirements and business workflows.
Enterprise-Grade Architecture
Unified by Red Hat OpenShift
Unified by Red Hat OpenShift, NxtGen delivers resilient orchestration, auto-scaling, and high availability across clusters, enabling seamless multi-GPU and multi-vendor GPU scheduling while ensuring reliable, containerized AI workloads at enterprise scale.
Single Application Services Platform
NxtGen’s Single Application Services Platform supports the complete build, deploy, secure, and manage lifecycle, enabling seamless scaling of AI applications from development to enterprise production with built-in security, governance, and performance.
