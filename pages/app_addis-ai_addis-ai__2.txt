Addis AI gives developers a unified infrastructure layer for text generation, speech recognition, natural voices, translation, multimodal reasoning, and realtime voice, built to process African languages as first-class inputs. Why Addis AI?
Building AI for Africa requires language-specific training. Many general-purpose models have limited exposure to the morphology, scripts, and cultural context of African languages. Addis AI treats those languages as the core product.
Why language-specific infrastructure matters
Morphology: Amharic verbs fuse subject, object, and tense into single words. This complex structure requires language-specific training. Our models parse it correctly, while major general-purpose AI models can misinterpret or hallucinate the underlying meaning.
Culture: Our models understand African history, regional geography, and contemporary legal and regulatory contexts.
Voice: We don&#x27;t just do text. We built the most natural sounding TTS engines for Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, Swahili, Hausa, Sidama and Wolaytta.
The Core Engines
Choose the capability your product needs, then follow the corresponding guide to start building.
Addis-፩-አሌፍ (NLP)
Our flagship Large Language Model. It is optimized for instruction following, reasoning, and content generation.
Best for: Chatbots, RAG (Document Search), and Summarization.
Languages: Amharic, Afaan Oromo and Tigrinya are fully supported, and the model also understands English instructions.
Context Window: 128k tokens.
አሌፍ-Audio (Voice)
A suite of high-fidelity audio models.
Addis Voices 2 New : The latest generation of Addis AI&#x27;s Text-to-Speech technology, with multiple production-ready Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, Swahili, Hausa, Sidama and Wolaytta voices designed for natural, expressive delivery. Use the catalog, previews, cost estimates, and durable clips to build conversational AI, narration, customer support, announcements, and other voice-first applications. Explore the complete Text-to-Speech guide .
አሌፍ-Audio-AM : Amharic Text-to-Speech with natural intonation.
አሌፍ-Audio-OM : Afaan Oromo Text-to-Speech.
አሌፍ-stt : Speech-to-Text transcription that handles dialect variations.
The previous Voice 1 Text-to-Speech API remains available only for existing applications and is documented in the Legacy Text-to-Speech guide .
Realtime Audio Deprecated
Powered by አሌፍ-1.2-realtime-audio , which is deprecated and kept only for existing applications. For new voice agents, use the Streaming voice agent guide built on Speech-to-Text and Addis Voices 2.
Latency: <300ms response time.
Use Case: Live conversational agents (like Siri/Alexa) that need to interrupt and respond instantly.
