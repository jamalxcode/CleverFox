Adding a card
Log in to the dashboard and click on Usage & Billing . On the billing page, click on Add new .
When adding a card, a hold of $5 is automatically placed on your card. The amount is automatically refunded after 7 days.
Adding Credits with Crypto or Card
Credits
Credits are automatically applied to future invoices. When an invoice is paid in full or in part with credits, it will be displayed on your invoice as a discount applied.
Credits can only be purchased if the team’s currency is USD.
To purchase credits, navigate to Usage & Billing > Billing , locate the Credits section, and click on Add credits .
Using Credit Card
In the Credit Card tab, enter the amount you wish to add and click Continue .
Ensure you have a default credit card set up.
Using Crypto
In the Crypto tab, enter the amount you wish to add.
Choose between the Solana or Ethereum blockchain and click Continue .
Ensure you have a wallet compatible with the selected blockchain.
Payments will be processed in USDC tokens.
After payment, credits will be added immediately to your account. They can be verified in Usage & Billing > Billing under the Credits section.
