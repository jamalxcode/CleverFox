FAQ Frequently asked questions
Can&#x27;t find what you&#x27;re looking for? Reach out to our support team .
How do I rent a GPU from Jarvislabs? Sign up, top-up your credits, select your preferred GPU (e.g., A100, H100), and launch your instance. Within minutes, you’ll have a fully configured environment ready for AI training or any GPU-accelerated workload.
Do you offer a free trial? We don’t offer a free trial. The minimum credit purchase is $25 for international customers and ₹500 for customers billed in India. Our pay-as-you-go model ensures you only pay when you rent a GPU and use it.
How am I charged for GPU usage? You’re charged only for the time your rented GPU instance is running. When paused, you pay solely for storage. Our per-second billing means no hidden fees or commitments.
What is a template? A template is a pre-built container with a primary framework (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow). After you rent a GPU, you can install custom packages via apt or pip to suit your project.
What is the difference between a Template and a VM? A template is a managed container — we handle the OS, drivers, CUDA, and the runtime, and you bring your code. A VM is a full machine with root access from minute one, so you can run your own Docker or Kubernetes, load custom kernels and drivers, and install anything a container can’t host. Both use the same GPUs at the same hourly rate.
Can I get root access on a GPU instance? Yes — launch a VM. You get root SSH immediately and full control of the operating system, including custom kernel modules, your own container runtime, and your own monitoring or security agents.
Can I use models without renting a GPU? Yes. Token Factory gives you access to hosted open models through an OpenAI-compatible API. Choose a model in the dashboard, try it in the playground and pay for input and output tokens.
Do you offer serverless GPU inference? Yes. Bring your own model on vLLM, SGLang, or Ollama and get an endpoint URL backed by an autoscaling worker pool that scales to zero, with logs, metrics, and usage built in. You pay per second of worker runtime, and you can deploy one from the dashboard after signing up.
What are Managed Endpoints, and how do they differ from Serverless? Managed Endpoints are open models from our catalog that you deploy as an API endpoint. We select and maintain the GPU configuration, serving image, precision, and kernels; you choose the model and how it scales. Serverless is the same autoscaling worker pool with your own serving stack, for when you need control over the framework and model. Both bill per second of worker runtime.
Can I get a multi-node GPU cluster? Yes. Launch an Instant Cluster from the dashboard, with NVIDIA H200 available today and more GPU options being added. For 128, 256, 1,024 GPUs and beyond, reserve a cluster built on NVIDIA reference architecture with InfiniBand; our sales team confirms capacity and delivery dates.
Can I run my GPUs on a private network? Yes. You can create a VPC with your own private IP ranges and run your instances inside it, isolated from other tenants’ traffic. VPCs are currently available in our India regions.
How quickly can I start using my GPU? Once you rent a GPU, instances typically launch in seconds. Start coding immediately via JupyterLab, VS Code Web, or SSH.
How is my data protected? Your rented GPU instances run in isolated environments with private networking, ensuring proper data isolation and security.
What happens when I pause my instance? Pausing stops GPU usage charges. You pay only for storage while it’s paused. Resume anytime to continue working where you left off.
What happens when I delete/destroy my instance? Deletion is permanent, and data cannot be recovered. After deletion, no further charges apply.
What regions do you support for renting GPUs? We currently offer GPU rentals in India, with more regions coming soon.
What kind of support is included? We provide technical support related to the entire lifecycle of your rented GPU instance. While we do our best to address framework-specific queries, we can’t guarantee support for all open-source tools.
How are payments handled? We use Stripe for secure payments. Pay with credit/debit cards or net banking, ensuring a smooth transaction process.
Do you support UPI, Paypal, etc? Not currently. We support Stripe transactions at this time.
What is the refund policy? We don’t offer refunds. If you have a valid reason, email us at engineering@jarvislabs.ai, and we’ll review your request.
