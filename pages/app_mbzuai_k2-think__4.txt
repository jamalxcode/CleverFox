Clear reasoning. Confident answers with K2 Horizon.
K2 Horizon gives you access to MBZUAI’s advanced AI reasoning capabilities.
Ask questions, explore ideas, and work through complex challenges with clear, thoughtful AI-generated responses.
Features
• Text chat — Enter questions or prompts and receive AI-generated responses.
• Chat history — View, search, and continue your previous conversations.
• Secure sign-in — Sign in with your account, Google to sync chats across devices.
How it works
1. Tap “Sign in” and choose your preferred sign-in method.
2. Type a prompt to ask a question or provide instructions.
3. Read through the AI-generated response.
4. Open chat history from the top-left icon, or swipe from the left edge, to revisit past conversations.
5. Use “Regenerate” on a message whenever you want a different response.
All listed features are available after sign-in. K2 Horizon has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
