Triple data replication is included in the cost
A fault-tolerant network disk cluster holds your data. Every block is triple copied and kept on separate servers in different racks. In the event that one copy becomes unavailable, the system will automatically fall back to the other two, ensuring continuous access.
Server freezing for cost saving
This feature allows you to temporarily shut down your server, avoiding charges for vCPU, RAM, and GPU (if applicable). This option is available in the control panel, via OpenStack CLI or Terraform for servers with a bootable network volume.
Streamlined management via a unified control panel or API

What types of tasks are well-suited for cloud hosting?
Cloud servers are suitable for a variety of tasks, including:
— Supporting both low-traffic (like data processing) and high-traffic web applications (such as e-commerce and large databases)
— Developing and hosting websites and applications
— Streamlining the CI/CD process to alleviate the workload of developers
— Managing repositories and microservices, as well as development and testing environments
— Hosting content management systems
— Facilitating application clustering
— Offloading business operations (for instance, maintaining backup copies)
— Ensuring that data storage and processing comply with local regulations and international security protocols
— Creating and administering cloud databases
— Quickly scaling digital services
— Deploying virtual desktop environments, among others.
For more in-depth guidance on the functionality of cloud servers and the types of tasks they can handle, please reach out to our technical support team at hello@servercore.com . We will contact you within one business day.

What advantages does cloud hosting offer for your business?
The benefits of utilizing cloud servers encompass:
— Rapid scalability for digital services
— Flexible configurations that allow for quick adjustments and custom setups
— High availability of services with assured computing resources
— Secure data storage located in data centers equipped with backup systems for power, communication, and cooling
— Payment strictly for the computing resources utilized
— Cost savings on purchasing and maintaining physical server equipment, along with staffing a maintenance team
— Exceptional server performance powered by cutting-edge Intel ® Xeon ® and AMD EPYC™ processors.
I’m uncertain about how to select the right cloud server setup for my business. What steps should I take?
Contact our technical support team by emailing your inquiries to hello@servercore.com or by submitting a ticket through the panel. We will assist you in finding a server that meets your disk and memory requirements within one business day.
Сan I change my cloud hosting setup and obtain more cloud resources if needed?
Feel free to adjust the virtual machine (VM) and increase its resources at any point, which will only require a few minutes of your time. Detailed instructions can be found in the documentation .
How is the pricing for server rentals determined?
The total cost is determined by the resource usage of the cloud server, including the quantity of cores, memory, and local disk space. You can use the calculator on our website to estimate the price.
What is the process for paying for cloud hosting rentals?
You have the option to pay for server rentals using either your local currency or bonus credits. To make a payment, simply replenish your account balance in the control panel. Our cloud platform operates on a pay-as-you-go basis, where charges are incurred hourly based on the usage of computing resources and external traffic from the prior hour.
For more detailed information about the payment procedure for cloud servers, please refer to the documentation .
Which operating systems can be installed on the cloud server, and is it possible for me to upload my own?
Preconfigured images of operating systems such as Ubuntu, CentOS, Debian, Fedora, Windows, and Oracle Linux, complete with a ready-to-use environment and set parameters, can be found in the control panel.
If these options do not meet your needs, you have the ability to upload your own image via the control panel. For further details on this process, please consult the documentation .
