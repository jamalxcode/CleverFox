What do I get with the Free (Standard) plan?
Search history access
Practically unlimited basic searches
Very limited amount of Pro Searches
Perplexity picks the best model for your query
Basic file uploads (limited)
No access to advanced AI models, image generation, or premium support.
What does Perplexity Pro (Individual) offer?
Extended access to Pro Search
A limited number of Create files and apps queries every 30 days
Access to advanced AI models. For the full list, see What advanced AI models are included in my subscription?
Image and video generation
Increased rate limits for file and attachment uploads and analysis
Exclusive support channels
Up to 50 file uploads per project
Best for: Anyone who wants the latest AI productivity and research tools.
What does Education Pro offer?
Education Pro is offered to verified students and educators at a discount. It includes everything in Pro — access to Perplexity Computer, access to Learn Mode , unlimited Pro Searches, file and image uploads, access to premium models, and more — plus education-specific tips and nudges.
Best for: Students or faculty who are looking to supercharge their teaching, learning, and maximize proven study methods for long-term understanding.
What does Perplexity Max (Individual) include?
Max includes everything in the Pro plan , plus:
Highest level of access to the full suite of advanced AI models: Get faster access to the latest models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and others in Perplexity Research, and create files and apps with minimal limits on volume or frequency.
Extended limits for Create files and apps queries: Create as many reports or projects as you need using advanced tools to create dashboards, spreadsheets, presentations, and web applications.
Early access to the newest products: Try the latest features and products before they’re available to everyone else.
Brain (Research Preview): Max subscribers using Perplexity Computer get access to Brain , a self-improving memory system that builds a working model of your projects, people, and files so Computer can act on what you’ve been doing. Free during the current preview period.
Priority support: Max subscribers receive the highest level of support, with dedicated infrastructure and faster response times.
Annual billing for Max is only available on perplexity.ai .
Best for: Users who need advanced access to Perplexity’s most powerful research features and early access to new features and products.
What does Enterprise Pro include?
All Pro features, plus:
Trust Center access and strict data privacy (data never used for training)
Per-seat pricing (starts at $40/month or $400/year per seat)
Advanced seat management for organizations
Internal knowledge search through shared Projects and centrally governed File Connectors
Increased daily file uploads (with organization-level controls)
Dedicated support
Administrators manage billing and account access
Projects for team collaboration, custom knowledge hubs, and internal file search.
Best for: Businesses and organizations needing robust collaboration, security, and administrator controls.
What does Enterprise Max offer?
All Enterprise Pro features, plus:
Create more files and apps: Generate the reports or projects you need with extended rate limits for advanced tools to build dashboards, spreadsheets, presentations, and web applications.
More Research queries: Generate comprehensive reports on complex topics, using autonomous, multi-step analysis.
Enhanced video generation: Enterprise Max users can generate 15 videos per month. Each video is 8 seconds long, includes audio, and is in landscape (16:9) format.
Advanced models in Perplexity Search: Enterprise Max users get the highest level of access to advanced models like GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 , with higher usage limits. This applies only within Perplexity on the web and not to programmatic access through the API , which is billed separately.
Greater file limits: Your personal repository (My Files) holds up to 10,000 files.
Premium security features: No seat minimum for organization-wide access to SCIM , Audit Logs , configurable Data Retention , and Insights dashboard if an organization has at least one Enterprise Max user.
Early access to new features: Try the latest features and products before they’re available to everyone else.
Priority support: Enterprise Max subscribers get faster support response times.
Best for: Businesses and organizations needing advanced data security, powerful collaboration tools, and the highest access to the latest AI models, Create files and apps and Research search modes, Comet Assistant, and Perplexity’s newest features.
What is the Perplexity API Platform for?
Designed for developers and businesses integrating Perplexity’s AI into their own products. Get started with the Agent API Quickstart .
Pay-as-you-go pricing: Buy credits or set up auto top-off
No access to advanced models or Pro features within the web/app interface
No complimentary API credits
No data logging or storage
Access to API projects for usage tracking and sharing API keys.
Best for: Developers needing API access for custom applications or automation.

How do I choose the right plan?
Choose Free if:
You’re a light user, just exploring, or need occasional answers.
Choose Pro if:
You want advanced AI, frequent research, file analysis, or image generation.
Choose Education Pro if:
You’re a current student or educator looking for ways to make learning more engaging and effective, and need extended access to Perplexity Academic .
Choose Max if:
You’re a power user who often does deep research on topics and wants to maximize the Create files and apps feature.
Choose Enterprise Pro if:
You’re a business or organization needing collaboration, security, and administrator controls.
Choose Enterprise Max if:
You’re a business or organization requiring Enterprise-grade security, control and collaboration tools, and would benefit from Perplexity’s most advanced plan, including the highest access to Create files and apps and Research search modes.
Choose the API Platform if:
You’re a developer integrating Perplexity into your own products.
Frequently asked questions
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time in your account settings. For Perplexity Pro, cancel before deleting your account to avoid being charged.
Is my data private?
Pro, Education Pro, and Max: You can opt out of data collection in your settings.
Enterprise Pro and Enterprise Max: Your data is never logged or used for training. Administrators can enforce strict privacy and organization-level controls.
API Platform: Your data is never logged or used for training.
Are there discounts for students, educational institutions, nonprofits, or government agencies?
Yes. Perplexity offers a 25% discount on Enterprise Pro and Enterprise Max seats for eligible educational institutions (not covered by the standard Student Program), nonprofit organizations, and government agencies. To apply, complete the discount request form using your organization email.
What support is available?
Free: Basic Help Center
Pro, Education Pro, and Max: Priority support through Discord and Help > Contact Support
Enterprise Pro and Enterprise Max: Dedicated support
Trial and promotional subscriptions: payment method required
If you’ve redeemed a trial or promotional subscription (for example, through a partner promotion), a valid payment method is required to activate and maintain your access.
Overall requirements
A valid credit or debit card is required for all promotional and trial redemptions.
No charges are applied during your promotional/trial period.
Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will auto-renew at the standard rate for the plan you selected during redemption (monthly or annual).
New users vs. existing users
New users are prompted to add a payment method during the sign-up and redemption flow.
Existing promotional/trial users who receive a payment method prompt must add a credit or debit card within 7 days of the original notification. If no card is added within that window, your account will be downgraded to Perplexity’s free version. Once you add a valid card, your promotional access resumes immediately. Any missed days will not be added back to your trial period.
Where notifications appear
Email: You will receive an email notification prompting you to add a payment method.
In-app: An in-app banner will appear prompting you to add your payment information.
How do I update my payment information?
Web (desktop):
1
Go to perplexity.ai/settings/account .
2
Scroll to the Subscription section.
3
Click Manage Plan (or Manage Subscription ).
4
Under Billing and Shipping Information , add or update your payment method.
Do not update your Perplexity payment information through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Doing so creates a new, separate subscription and you will be charged. Always update your payment method on the Perplexity website.
Need more help?
Contact us via Help > Contact Support . Pro subscribers can access live chat in Settings.
