Payment methods
You can use any of below payment methods:
Credit card . Pay using all popular credit (and debit) cards, including those issued by Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, and UnionPay.
Google Pay . Pay using a credit card, debit card, or PayPal through your Google Pay account. Paying with your Google Pay balance or any associated bank accounts are not supported at this time.
👍
To add a PayPal account to your Google Pay account, you currently must do so through the Google Play Store . PayPal cannot currently be added through the Google Pay website or mobile apps. When adding PayPal through Google Pay, it appears as a Discover Card within Cloud Manager and the Linode API .
PayPal . Pay using your PayPal balance, a credit or debit card, or bank account through your PayPal account.
Check, ACH, or wire transfer . Contact support if you wish to pay through one of these methods.
See Adding a new payment method for instructions on adding a new payment method to your account.
Add a new payment method
New payment methods can be added to an account through Cloud Manager . You can have up to six active payment methods on your account, including credit cards, Google Pay, and PayPal.
📘
Parent and child accounts
If your account is in the parent and child relationship , this operation is unavailable on a child account.
In Cloud Manager navigate to Administration > Billing . See Accessing billing information for more information.
If you&#x27;re directly adding a credit card, it needs to match the account&#x27;s billing contact information and address. Review the Billing Contact section and edit it if necessary (see Update billing contact information for more information).
In the Payment Method section, click Add Payment Method . The Add Payment Method panel opens.
Complete the form according to the payment method you want to add:
Pay with a credit card . Enter the credit card number, expiration date, and the security code (CVV) of the new card. Then click Add Credit Card .
👍
When the payment processor verifies your credit card, your bank may place a $1 authorization hold on your account.
Pay with Google Pay . Click Google Pay to open Google&#x27;s own Google Pay form. Log in to your Google account and select the payment method you want to use. Complete the form as necessary and click Continue , to be taken back to Cloud Manager .
Pay with PayPal . Click PayPal to open PayPal&#x27;s own form. Log in to your PayPal account and select the credit card or bank account you want to use. Fill out the form as necessary and click Save and Continue to be taken back to Cloud Manager .
We&#x27;ll add you&#x27;re selected payment method and assign it as the default payment method for future recurring payments. You can change this as necessary.
📘
This process doesn&#x27;t immediately charge any past due balance on the account to the new payment method. If you have an outstanding balance, you need to make a manual payment to bring your account up to date. See Make a one-time payment for more information.
Remove a payment method
To delete a payment method from your account, follow the instructions below:
In Cloud Manager navigate to Administration > Billing . See Accessing billing information for more information.
Under the Payment Methods section, locate the payment method you wish to remove.
Click the corresponding ellipsis menu and select Delete from the dropdown menu. If the payment method is the default payment method on the account, the Delete option will be disabled and you&#x27;ll first need to change the default method.
View and change the default payment method
When an invoice is generated, the invoice amount and any past-due balance are charged to your default payment method. To view or change your default payment method, follow these instructions:
In Cloud Manager navigate to Administration > Billing . See Accessing billing information for more information.
In the Payment Methods section, your default payment method has the Default label next to its last 4 digits and expiration date.
To change your default payment method, click the ellipsis icon next to a payment method and select Make Default .
