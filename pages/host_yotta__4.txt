Shakti Cloud offers 2 procurement models, providing customers the flexibility to either purchase Standard Product or adopt a Bare Metal first approach with
optional add-ons.
A. Standard Product Purchase
Customers may directly procure services by navigating to the relevant product sections; AI Lab, AI Workspace VM, Clusters, & Shakti Studio. These purchases include the core service along with the required infrastructure and orchestration layers. Customers may further enhance their setup by adding ancillary services such as connectivity, storage, and security layers.
B. Bare Metal First Procurement
Alternatively, customers may choose to procure Bare Metal Nodes as a standalone purchase. They can select and enable the required services from the AI Support Services section such as AI Lab, Clusters, & Shakti Studio. Additional options such as connectivity, ancillary services, and security layers can also be added on top of the provisioned infrastructure.
