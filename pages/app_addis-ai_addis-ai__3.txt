Talk to Addis AI in your own language! Instantly access Amharic and Oromiffa voice assistants, smart chat, and local-language support.
Addis AI lets you chat or speak naturally in Amharic and Afan Oromo. Use your voice or type messages and get smart, helpful replies in your language.
Features include:
• Voice assistant with spoken and written responses
• Real-time translation for text and audio
• Upload documents (PDF, DOC, TXT) to get summaries or answers
• Send images for AI-generated descriptions
• Support for both Amharic and Afan Oromo languages
• Regular updates improving UI, stability, and language support
Whether you want quick answers, language help, or just to chat, Addis AI makes it simple and intuitive.
