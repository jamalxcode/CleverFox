About this app
arrow_forward
Addis AI is building voice-first AI infrastructure for African languages.
This app gives you direct access to Addis AI’s speech and language models in Amharic and Afaan Oromo through voice, chat, translation, and multimodal features.
With Addis AI, you can:
• Speak and chat in Amharic and Afaan Oromo
• Ask questions using voice
• Translate between Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and English
• Generate and work with images
• Get help with writing, learning, ideas, and everyday questions
The same underlying speech and language technology is also available to developers through Addis AI’s APIs and official SDKs.
Learn more:
https://addisassistant.com
