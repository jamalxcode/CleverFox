What Payment Methods Do You Accept?
1 min read Updated 26 October 2026
0 comments
Check which payment methods Vultr accepts: major cards, BitPay crypto, PayPal, Alipay, and UnionPay.
Vultr supports a wide range of global payment methods to ensure flexibility and accessibility for users worldwide. To learn how to fund your account and keep services active, see the Make Account Payments guide . The following payment options are currently accepted:
Credit and Debit Cards : Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and JCB.
Digital Currencies via BitPay : Bitcoin (BTC), Bitcoin Cash (BCH), Ethereum (ETH), Dogecoin (DOGE), Paxos (PAX), Binance USD (BUSD), Litecoin (LTC), USD Coin (USDC), and Gemini Dollar (GUSD).
Third-party Payment Platforms : PayPal, Alipay, and UnionPay.
PayPal may also support additional local payment networks depending on your region, such as Giropay and bank transfers.
Note
To manage or update your payment method, visit the Billing section in the Vultr Console .
